  • Full business directory profile
  • Access to general chamber training workshops

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $300 / year

  • Special business spotlight on social media channels
  • Access to specialized B2B roundtables

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $500 / year

  • All Silver tier benefits
  • Free access to all training and seminars
  • Permanent homepage spotlight advertisement position
  • VIP invitations to private business summits and expos
